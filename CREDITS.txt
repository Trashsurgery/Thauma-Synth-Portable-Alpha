Thauma: credits
===============

Thauma's own code, shaders, looks and documents are by Athron (MIT, see LICENSE). This file
names, in plain words, every piece of other people's work that Thauma uses or learned from,
and where its licence is. The full text of every licence is in THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.txt.
Shader packs that ship as whole files keep their authors' headers and have a CREDITS.txt of
their own in their folder (custom-shaders/, transitions/, testdata/isf-tests/).

Libraries linked into Thauma
----------------------------
GLFW (window and input), zlib/libpng licence
GLEW (OpenGL loading), modified BSD, MIT and Khronos licences
Spout2 (sharing the picture with other programs), BSD 2-Clause
nlohmann/json (reading and writing looks and settings), MIT
RtMidi (MIDI), MIT-style licence
MinGW-w64 winpthreads and the GCC runtime, their own permissive licences (texts in the licence file)
Dear ImGui (the control panel), MIT
Quicksand font, SIL Open Font Licence (Fonts/OFL.txt)
FFmpeg (reading video files), LGPL 2.1 or later, built without any GPL parts; its source is in FFmpeg/source

Maths and ideas inside Thauma's built-in effects
------------------------------------------------
Each borrowed function carries a one-line credit in the shader above it.
Rush, Whirl, Grade: pixi-filters (zoom-blur, twist, adjustment), MIT
Strata: rasterwarp (colorize banding), MIT
Veil, Tilt: glsl-fast-gaussian-blur (13-tap Gaussian weights), MIT
Phosphor: crt-lottes (public domain), crt-hyllian (MIT) and crt-geom-mini (MIT) from the libretro
    glsl-shaders collection, permissive files only
Nitrate: pixi-filters old-film (MIT); film_noise from libretro glsl-shaders (public domain)
Dropout: ntsc-rs (MIT); tiny_ntsc from libretro glsl-shaders (MIT)
Judder, Scramble: KinoGlitch by Keijiro Takahashi (MIT); Kino by Keijiro Takahashi (Unlicense)
Lance, Inkwash, Shear, Hone: Kino by Keijiro Takahashi (Unlicense)
Shafts: WebGL-Fluid-Simulation by Pavel Dobryakov (sun rays), MIT
Tincture, Swish, Pebble: pixi-filters (color-map, radial-blur, motion-blur, shockwave), MIT
Mire: KinoDatamosh by Keijiro Takahashi, MIT as its file headers state

Maths inside Thauma's sound analysis and controls
------------------------------------------------
Sharp kick, Snare and Hats: the hit finding of krotala-core (Athron's Krotala project), MIT
Listen (the tempo by ear): the tempo finding of krotala-core, MIT
Follow Krotala: the OSC contract of krotala-core, MIT; receiver implemented in Thauma
XY pad puck (src/xy_pad.cpp): ported from krotala-core, MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 Athron
Only the MIT core is used; none of Krotala's GPL app code. One complete notice covers these uses.
